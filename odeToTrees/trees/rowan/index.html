Rowan

(Sorbus aucuparia)

"Rowan tree and red thread, Make the witches tine their speed."

T In British and Scandinavian folklore, the rowan tree is revered as a powerful protector against evil and witchcraft. Its association with protection is ancient and widespread.

I The tree's most recognizable feature is its clusters of bright red or orange berries in late summer and autumn. These berries are an important food source for many birds. The leaves are pinnate, meaning they are arranged like a feather, with pairs of leaflets growing along a central stem.
