Hawthorn

(Crataegus monogyna)

"Ne'er cast a clout till May is out"

T he hawthorn is also known as the 'May-tree' because of its profusion of creamy-white blossom that blooms in that month. This is the origin of the old saying, "Ne'er cast a clout till May is out" which refers to the hawthorn blossom, not the month itself.

I t's dense, thorny branches provide excellent nesting sites for birds and its bright red berries, known as 'haws', are a vital food source for wildlife throughout the autumn and winter. Its hard, fine-grained wood has been used for everything from tool handles to walking sticks, and it is a key component of our traditional hedgerows, acting as a living boundary.
